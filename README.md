### 🚀 Featured Projects

**🛰️ [OrbitalOps AI — Satellite Mission Operations Platform](https://github.com/SachiTiwari04/orbitalops-ai)**
Full-stack mission operations platform for satellite telemetry monitoring, anomaly detection, predictive analysis, and mission simulation, built during a Def-Space internship at BSERC. React dashboards connected to FastAPI REST APIs and PostgreSQL, with a mission intelligence pipeline for trend analysis and recommendation generation.
`React` `TypeScript` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL`

**💰 [SmartCents — Personal Finance Assistant](https://github.com/SachiTiwari04/SmartCents)**
Personal finance app combining expense tracking, real-time market data, and personalized financial guidance powered by the OpenAI API, with gamified savings features.
`React` `Firebase` `OpenAI API` `Alpha Vantage API` `NewsAPI`

Farm management app for tracking livestock health, feeding, and vaccination records, featuring ML-based fecal analysis for early disease detection and an OpenAI-powered assistant for contextual farmer support.
`Firebase` `Google Maps API` `OpenAI API`
